How impact flows

  • A depends on B, A runs on B, A hosted on B: if B fails, A is impacted.
  • A part of B: if A fails, B (e.g. its business domain) is impacted.
  • A connects to B: drawn on the topology, no impact propagation.
  • Relationships that would create a dependency loop are refused.
